&nbsp; Search Service Firmware Software and Firmware Updates for Cameras ALEXA 35 SUP 6.1.0 ALEXA 35 / ALEXA 35 Xtreme / ALEXA 35 Live / ALEXA 35 Live Xtreme SUP 6.1.0 July 7, 2026 We are pleased to introduce Software Update Package SUP 6.1.0. for all camera models in the ALEXA 35 family: ALEXA 35, ALEXA 35 Xtreme, ALEXA 35 Live, and ALEXA 35 Live Xtreme. This release introduces Picture-in-Picture Tracking Zoom, a new variation of the Tracking Zoom feature first introduced for the ALEXA 35 Xtreme in SUP 5.2.0. It allows operators to use Tracking Zoom while maintaining a full view of the scene, with the tracked and zoomed image displayed in a picture-in-picture window. The ALEXA 35 Xtreme now also supports high-speed image transmission via its dual 12G-SDI outputs, delivering frame rates of up to 120 fps in UHD and 480 fps in HD. This enhancement makes the camera ideally suited for high-speed capture workflows in live broadcast environments. In addition, all cameras in the family now support the use of external GPS modules, allowing GPS position data to be recorded directly in clip metadata. New Features in SUP 6.1.0 High Frame Rate SDI* GPS Metadata Picture-in-Picture Tracking Zoom** SDI Tally Overlay Extended Genlock Support Please review the release notes and consult the ALEXA 35 User Manual for SUP 6.1.0 to ensure optimal operation. We recommend that all users update to this version.
